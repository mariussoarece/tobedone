Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or restore my data?
Open Settings › Data & Backup in the app. Backups are also visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › ToBeDone.

I don't get notifications.
Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › ToBeDone and turn on Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners. In the app, check Settings › Notifications.
