Everything in one place

Three simple sections, so you always know where things go.

PLAN

  • To Do: due date, time, priority and a reminder
  • Monthly Expenses: recurring payments with a running total in your local currency
  • Projects: tasks with subtasks you complete

DO

  • Shopping: product lists with prices and the total still to buy
  • Grocery: never forget what to buy

GROW

  • Daily Routine: a monthly calendar and a weekly timeline
  • Confidence: check-ins with your 30-day trend
  • Focus Test: train your attention span

A reminder every evening

At the time you choose, ToBeDone lists what's still unfinished. If you finished everything, it stays silent.

On time, every time

Get a notification when each Daily Routine activity starts and when a task should already be completed.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone, backed up automatically. No account, no ads, no stress.
