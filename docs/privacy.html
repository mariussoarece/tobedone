ToBeDone – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Summary

ToBeDone does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, shopping and grocery lists, monthly expenses, projects, daily activities, confidence entries, focus test results and your name are saved only on your device, inside the app. The app also keeps automatic backup files in its own folder on your device (visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › ToBeDone). We have no servers and cannot see this information.

Notifications

Reminders and the daily summary are local notifications created on your iPhone. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Export and import

When you choose Export, the app creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice. Import only reads files you pick yourself.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party services.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data and backups. You can also delete items inside the app at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
